Atmósfera Estándar Internacional (ISA)

$$ T(h) = T_0 - L \cdot h \quad \text{(troposfera, h ≤ 11 km)} $$
$$ p(h) = p_0 \left( \frac{T(h)}{T_0} \right)^{\frac{g_0}{R\,L}} $$
$$ \rho = \frac{p}{R\,T} $$
T₀ = 288.15 K, p₀ = 101325 Pa, L = 0.0065 K/m, g₀ = 9.80665 m/s², R = 287.058 J/(kg·K)

Propiedades del aire (estándar frío)

$$ c_v = \frac{R}{k-1} \qquad c_p = k\,c_v \qquad R = 287.058 \; \tfrac{J}{kg\,K} $$

Combustión y calor aportado

$$ \text{C}_n\text{H}_m + \left(n + \tfrac{m}{4}\right)\text{O}_2 \rightarrow n\,\text{CO}_2 + \tfrac{m}{2}\,\text{H}_2\text{O} $$
$$ AFR_{est} = \frac{(n + m/4)(1+3{,}76)\;M_{aire}}{M_{comb}} $$
$$ AFR_{real} = \lambda \cdot AFR_{est} \qquad q_{in} = \frac{PCI}{AFR_{real}} \; \left[\tfrac{J}{kg\,aire}\right] $$

Ciclo Otto · 1→2 compresión, 2→3 Q a V=cte, 3→4 expansión, 4→1 rechazo

$$ T_2 = T_1\,r^{k-1} \qquad p_2 = p_1\,r^{k} $$
$$ T_3 = T_2 + \frac{q_{in}}{c_v} \qquad p_3 = p_2 \frac{T_3}{T_2} $$
$$ \eta_{Otto} = 1 - \frac{1}{r^{\,k-1}} $$

Ciclo Diesel · 2→3 Q a P=cte

$$ T_3 = T_2 + \frac{q_{in}}{c_p} \qquad r_c = \frac{v_3}{v_2} = \frac{T_3}{T_2} $$
$$ \eta_{Diesel} = 1 - \frac{1}{r^{k-1}} \cdot \frac{r_c^{\,k} - 1}{k\,(r_c - 1)} $$

Ciclo Sabathé (dual) · 2→3 Q a V=cte, 3→4 Q a P=cte

$$ \alpha = \frac{p_3}{p_2} \qquad r_c = \frac{v_4}{v_3} $$
$$ \eta_{Sab} = 1 - \frac{1}{r^{k-1}} \cdot \frac{\alpha\,r_c^{\,k} - 1}{(\alpha - 1) + k\,\alpha\,(r_c - 1)} $$

Trabajo, PME y dimensionamiento

$$ w_{net} = q_{in} - q_{out} \qquad PME = \frac{w_{net}}{v_1 - v_2} $$
$$ V_{total} = \frac{P}{PME \cdot \frac{rpm}{60\,\tau}} \quad \text{con } \tau = \tfrac{\text{tiempos}}{2} $$
$$ V_{unit} = \frac{\pi}{4}\,D^{2}\,S \qquad D = \sqrt[3]{\frac{4\,V_{unit}}{\pi\,(S/D)}} $$
